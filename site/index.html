Get help with your family's paperwork. Keep it private.

familybox is a small box that lives at your home. It lets an AI helper like ChatGPT answer questions about your family's papers, without ever handing the papers over.

How it works

Three simple steps.

  1. Put your papers in the box

    Take a photo of a card or form, or add a file. They stay in the box, at home.

  2. Ask your AI helper a question

    Use ChatGPT or Claude like you already do. The helper asks the box, and never sees the papers.

  3. Private numbers show only on your phone

    The helper gets a safe answer. If you need the whole number, tap once and it shows on your phone.

To be honest about the 0 to 4: a few times the AI hinted at a health detail without naming it, almost always to the person it belonged to. No Social Security number, passport number or ID ever got out. We're still improving this.

What we promise

  • Your papers stay in your box. AI helpers never get a copy.
  • The AI that reads them is locked, so no one can look inside. The box checks the lock every time.
  • A full number only shows on its owner's phone.
  • If anything looks wrong, you get no answer instead of an unsafe one.

What we can't promise

  • Safety from someone who has the password to the box itself.
  • That a clever hint can never slip through.
  • What happens to a number after you show it on your own phone.
