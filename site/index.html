How it works

Your papers stay home. You still get help.

Social Security cards, tax forms, passports and health records stay in the box. The AI helper can ask the box questions, but it never sees or keeps the papers themselves.

  1. Put your papers in the box

    Take a photo of a card or form, or add a file.

  2. Ask your AI helper

    Use ChatGPT or Claude like you already do.

  3. Private numbers show only on your phone

    The helper gets a safe answer. One tap shows you the rest.

Is it safe?

We tried hard to break it.

We used a made-up family and tried 39 different tricks to get private details out, three times over. Here is how many got through.

About the 0 to 4: a few times the AI hinted at a health detail without naming it, almost always to the person it belonged to. No Social Security number, passport number or ID ever got out. We're still improving this.

What we promise

  • Your papers stay in your box. AI helpers never get a copy.
  • The AI that reads them is locked, so no one can look inside.
  • A full number only shows on its owner's phone.
  • If anything looks wrong, you get no answer instead of an unsafe one.

What we can't promise

  • Safety from someone who has the password to the box itself.
  • That a clever hint can never slip through.
  • What happens to a number after you show it on your own phone.
